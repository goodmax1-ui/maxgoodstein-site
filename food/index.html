Tips

  • Wash all greens once when you get home; dry well, store with a paper towel.
  • Keep avocados on the counter until they yield to gentle pressure, then move to the fridge.
  • Cook fresh fish within 1–2 days of buying; freeze if it'll sit longer.
  • Pre-cook the chicken thighs and ribeye on the day you buy them — leftovers carry the week.
  • Hard-boil a few eggs on Sunday for fast salad protein.
