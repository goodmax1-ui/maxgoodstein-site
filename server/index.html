About this setup

What is all this?
A MacBook Pro at the apartment runs everything on this page, always on, in Docker containers. Media, photos, and audio live on a 4.5TB external drive. The Mac also answers DNS for the whole apartment and the tailnet, which is what makes the ad blocking work everywhere.
Addresses to remember
  • Tailnet (works from anywhere): 100.117.16.114, hostname apt
  • Home wifi only: 192.168.0.137
  • Every service is that address plus the port shown on its card
Something's broken
  • Cards won't load? Open the Tailscale app and make sure it's connected.
  • Jellyfin, Immich, or Audiobookshelf empty? Check the easystore drive is plugged into the Mac.
  • Internet looks broken on everything at home? The Mac is down or asleep. Wake or restart it; DNS comes back with it.
  • One service acting up? SSH to the Mac and docker restart it.
  • The full recovery guide lives in the encrypted Library above.
Setting up a new phone
Install Tailscale first and sign in, then the apps: Jellyfin (or JellyTV), Immich, Audiobookshelf, ntfy (subscribe to topic homelab), and Home Assistant. Point each at 100.117.16.114 with the port from its card.

Links only load on the tailnet. Not connected? Open the Tailscale app first.
